# Desktop motion comparison — owner correction, 5 November 2026

Codex scrolled the live desktop pages before issuing this refinement brief. This is observed reference direction, not a claim that Pathfolio already meets it.

## Observed references

- CodeSense: https://pr-reviewer-omega-liard.vercel.app/ — the hero has moving code fragments and depth; the review demo stages its content; a pinned story shrinks one code panel into a repository grid, changes its adjacent text, highlights related files, and draws connecting lines as the scroll advances. Lower sections continue the motion language.
- Grepless: https://grepless.vercel.app/ — clipped/staggered headline entrance, continuous semantic particles, query typing and embedding cells, angled moving language ribbon, staggered result cards, blur-to-sharp feature and repository cards, animated miniature diagrams. Motion extends through the page.
- Haan: https://haan-kolkata-pilot.pages.dev/ — split/masked headings, word-by-word scroll lighting, sweeping large text, bridge/car motion, large image reveals, pinned phone state changes, drawing route paths and moving marker, count/progress choreography, staggered detail rows and imagery. Background and foreground are active together.

## Actual Pathfolio gap

The background is accepted. Preserve its identity and palette. Current foreground is too static: headings and paragraphs simply scroll into view, large groups of cards remain visually unchanged, and the three measured chapter deltas affect a limited portion of each composition. Three passing transform checks do not establish the desired motion density or quality.

## Owner's revised acceptance direction

- Every visible content group needs deliberate animation: hero headline/lead/actions/ledger; each section heading and lead; pipeline stations; STAR fragments, tabs and citation; vault and comparison rows; product screenshots/captions; final actions and footer groups. Structural wrapper divs should not accumulate nested transforms that fight one another.
- Keep the current background and type identity, but make the foreground feel like a motion studio advertisement. Materially transform compositions during scroll; a few subtle tilts or adding the same fade to every section is insufficient.
- Include at least one prominent product-specific 3D sequence using perspective/depth and layered dossier paper. CSS/SVG 3D is acceptable if convincing; do not add heavy WebGL merely for a dependency name.
- Use a deliberate motion vocabulary: line/word masks; varied staggered reveals; pinned or long-scroll transformations; dramatic paper assembly/folding; depth/parallax product gallery; drawn rails/connectors; polished control feedback. Keep controls usable during motion.
- The opening must be visibly choreographed, and each ordinary desktop scroll should encounter foreground movement. Supporting guide/privacy content also needs coherent, restrained entry motion; the app is a separate task-focused surface.
- Mobile must remain readable without sticky scroll traps, overflow, intersecting stamps, or labels crossing text. Reduced-motion and JavaScript-failure states must expose all content. Preserve existing app/download/demo behavior.
- Build through Antigravity, default Gemini 3.8 Flash High. Do not use Argon. Sonnet Low is reserved for an actual stalled narrow fix under the owner's existing allowance.
- Update PRD, motion spec and review evidence; check local and live desktop/phone states, old chapter assertions, coverage of all content groups, reverse scrolling/reload at middle/hash jumps, and reduced-motion visibility. Keep benchmark observation separate from implemented evidence. Commit/push and deploy the finished revision.
