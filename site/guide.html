Pathfolio Strategy Guide

How to manage your application velocity, never miss recruiter follow-ups, and structure quantifiable engineering evidence that wins offers.

1. Navigating the 6-Stage Pipeline Lifecycle

Early-career technical recruiting requires organizing dozens of opportunities without losing context. Pathfolio standardizes every application into six discrete, audited stages:

  1. Saved: Target companies and open requisitions under research. Use this stage to gather internal referrals, note compensation bands, and tailor your portfolio focus before officially submitting.
  2. Applied: Application formally submitted. Record the exact submission timestamp, recruiter portal URL, and confirmation ID.
  3. Screening: Recruiter introductory call or automated coding assessment (e.g., CodeSignal, HackerRank). Prepare talking points on your recent technical projects.
  4. Interview: Active technical loops in progress (data structures & algorithms, systems architecture, live coding, and hiring manager behavioral sessions).
  5. Offer: Formal written offer extended. Track base compensation, equity grants, signing bonuses, and decision deadlines with complete local privacy.
  6. Rejected: Concluded applications. Keeping structured debrief notes helps identify patterns to improve in future hiring loops.

Automatic Timeline Tracking

Moving an opportunity between stages automatically logs a dated entry in the Activity History timeline so you can measure your cycle velocity and average days per stage.

2. Managing Action Items & Follow-Up Deadlines

Candidate momentum is lost when follow-ups are delayed. Pathfolio helps you stay ahead of recruiter communications and technical milestones:

  • Attach actionable tasks with target completion dates (e.g. "Send thank-you email to engineering manager", "Submit take-home system design repo").
  • Tasks past today's calendar date automatically surface on your dashboard under the Overdue Actions badge with high-contrast in-app indicators (visual on-screen indicators; Pathfolio does not use OS push notifications or background alarm services).
  • Completing a task instantly updates your pending badge counts and logs a completion event in your local activity history.

3. Structuring Quantifiable STAR Evidence Entries

During technical and behavioral rounds, engineering interviewers look for verified impact, not generic responsibilities. Use the Evidence Bank to store achievements formatted with the STAR principle:

Example STAR Evidence Item:

High-Throughput Telemetry Ingestion Engine

TAGS: Go, Kafka, Docker, Prometheus

Concise Result: Processed 22,000 events/sec with under 18ms p99 latency during peak university registration traffic, reducing cluster hosting cost by 35%.

You can cite this evidence entry across multiple job opportunities without duplicating files or copying paragraphs into multiple folders.

4. Atomic Versioned Backups & Device Transfer

Because Pathfolio operates entirely offline with client-side storage, your records live in your browser sandbox or Android app. To move data across devices or create safe checkpoints:

  • Go to Settings → Export Backup to generate a validated JSON schema v1 backup file.
  • To restore, select Import Backup: Pathfolio validates schema integrity, field types, and count statistics before replacing current data atomically.
  • All writes are committed as a single consistent snapshot, preventing partial corrupted state.
