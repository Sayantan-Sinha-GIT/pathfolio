Pathfolio User Guide

How to get the most out of your application pipeline, follow-up deadlines, and career evidence bank.

1. Navigating the Pipeline Lifecycle

Every role moves through six standardized stages:

  1. Saved: Roles you are researching, tailoring materials for, or requesting employee referrals on.
  2. Applied: Application officially submitted; waiting for recruiter acknowledgment.
  3. Screening: Recruiter initial chat or automated technical assessment link.
  4. Interview: Technical coding loops, system design sessions, or behavioral rounds in progress.
  5. Offer: Written offer extended; evaluating compensation, benefits, and decision deadlines.
  6. Rejected: Concluded application. Keep notes on what was learned for future cycles.

Tip: Moving an opportunity between stages automatically creates a dated entry in the Activity History timeline so you can track how long each interview cycle took.

2. Managing Follow-Up Deadlines

Recruiting momentum is lost when follow-ups are delayed. Pathfolio helps you stay on top of next actions:

  • Attach tasks with due dates (e.g. "Send thank-you email to engineering manager", "Complete take-home challenge").
  • Tasks past today's date automatically appear in the Overdue Actions badge on your dashboard.
  • Completing a task instantly updates your pending counts and logs a completion event.

3. Structuring Career Evidence Entries

During interviews, answering behavioral questions with quantifiable facts is critical. Use the Evidence Bank to store entries formatted with the STAR principle:

Example Evidence Item:

Distributed Telemetry Pipeline

Tags: Go, Kafka, Docker, Prometheus

Concise Result: Processed 15,000 events/sec with under 20ms p99 latency during peak university registration traffic, reducing cluster cost by 35%.

You can reference this entry across multiple job applications without having to duplicate text or re-upload files.
