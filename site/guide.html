Pathfolio Usage Guide

How to manage your application velocity, prepare reusable interview evidence, and keep your records safe on your own device.

1. Navigating the 6-Stage Pipeline Lifecycle

Early-career recruiting requires organizing dozens of opportunities without losing context. Pathfolio standardizes every application into six discrete, audited stages with zero telemetry:

  1. Saved: Target companies and open requisitions under research. Record company, role, source URL, work mode (remote, hybrid, on-site), and salary range note. Only company and role are required.
  2. Applied: Application formally submitted. Record the submission date, source URL, and recruiter contact note.
  3. Screening: Recruiter introductory chat or automated technical assessment. Set a next action date and attach follow-up reminders.
  4. Interview: Active technical and hiring-manager interview rounds. Attach reusable entries from your evidence bank so key achievements are ready.
  5. Offer: Formal offer extended. Track compensation, benefits notes, and response deadlines locally.
  6. Rejected: Archived application. Archived records leave active counts and remain searchable; you can restore them anytime.

Automatic Timeline Auditing

Every stage transition writes an entry into the record's activity timeline with its exact local date, creating an audit trail of your cycle duration.

2. Action Items & Follow-Up Deadlines

Candidate momentum stalls when follow-ups slip through the cracks. In Pathfolio, tasks stay tightly connected to opportunity records:

  • Attach actionable follow-ups with optional due dates (e.g. "Send thank-you email to lead engineer", "Review system architecture diagram").
  • Tasks due today or past due automatically surface on your dashboard under active alerts so nothing is missed.
  • Completing a task writes an entry to the opportunity's activity timeline and updates dashboard counters immediately.

3. The STAR Evidence Bank

In behavioral and technical interviews, interviewers assess verified achievements, not generic descriptions. The Evidence Bank stores reusable career proof structured around the STAR format:

Attach evidence references to opportunities without uploading documents or duplicating files. Removing an evidence entry never corrupts an opportunity's timeline.

4. Backup, Restore & Data Sovereignty

Pathfolio uses no cross-device cloud sync. Your data stays strictly on your device in local storage. To transfer records across devices or create safety checkpoints:

  • Export Backup: Downloads a versioned JSON backup conforming to Schema Version 1, including opportunities, tasks, evidence bank, and preferences.
  • Import Backup: Validates file size, schema version, and field types. Previews count breakdown before asking you to confirm replacement.
  • Atomic Replacement: Replaces existing data only upon user confirmation. If validation fails or you cancel, your current workspace is left untouched.
