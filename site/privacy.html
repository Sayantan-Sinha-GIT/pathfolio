Data Sovereignty & Local Privacy

Privacy Policy & Storage Model

Pathfolio is built on a simple principle: your career data belongs entirely to you. Nothing leaves your device unless you choose to export it.

1. Zero Telemetry & Zero Cloud Databases

Pathfolio contains no analytics SDKs, no behavioral trackers, no advertising beacons, and no backend database servers:

  • No accounts required: You never register, verify email addresses, or sign in with third-party identity providers.
  • No telemetry scripts: We do not track page views, feature clicks, search queries, or time spent in the app.
  • No server synchronization: We operate no cloud synchronization daemon, message broker, or telemetry collector.

2. Where Data Resides on Your Device

All application records reside exclusively in client-side persistence managed by the Flutter runtime:

  • Web App (`/app/`): Stored in your web browser's origin-isolated local storage sandbox. Data is accessible only to `pathfolio.pages.dev` on your browser profile.
  • Android Application: Stored in private, application-sandboxed key-value preferences. No external SD-card access or broad storage permissions are requested or used.
  • Encrypted Transport: Assets on Cloudflare Pages are served strictly over HTTPS with modern TLS 1.3 encryption.

3. Deliberate Data Movement (Export & Import)

Because there is no background cloud sync, data moves across devices only when you explicitly export a file:

Versioned JSON Schema (v1)

When you trigger Export Backup, the app generates a structured JSON file containing your opportunities, tasks, evidence bank entries, and preferences. You control where this file is saved, emailed, or archived.

When importing, Pathfolio validates the file structure and schema before prompting you to confirm replacement. If you reject the confirmation, your current workspace data remains intact.

4. Honest Storage Limits & Recommendations

Local-only storage gives you total confidentiality, but requires understanding its operating constraints:

Important Storage Fact: Browser storage is scoped to your browser profile. Clearing your browser's site data, using private/incognito windows, or switching browsers will not carry over existing records. Similarly, uninstalling the Android APK deletes its sandboxed app data.

We strongly recommend downloading a versioned JSON backup after significant milestones (e.g. after adding high-priority opportunities or recording interview rounds) and keeping it in your personal backup store.

5. Open Source Code Transparency

The entire source code of Pathfolio is published openly under the permissive MIT License on GitHub. You can inspect the implementation, verify the storage repository layer, compile the Flutter web app or Android APK yourself, and confirm that zero unauthorized outbound network connections are ever initiated.
